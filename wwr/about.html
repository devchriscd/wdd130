White Water Rafting

At White Water Rafting, our mission is to deliver unforgettable outdoor experiences through thrilling, safe, and professionally guided rafting trips. We are dedicated to connecting people with nature and creating lifelong memories on the water.

History

White Water Rafting began on the banks of the Salmon River in Idaho, when a small group of river guides decided to share the rivers they loved with everyone else. With two rafts and a borrowed trailer, they ran their first half-day trips for friends and neighbors.

Today our certified guides lead families, first-timers, and seasoned paddlers on trips ranging from gentle half-day floats to multi-day canyon expeditions. Safety, great gear, and a lot of fun are still at the heart of every trip we run.
