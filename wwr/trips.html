Our Trips

Every trip includes a certified guide, raft, paddles, helmet, and life jacket. Pick your pace, from a calm family float to a full-on whitewater challenge.

Half Day Adventure

Perfect for families and first-timers. Spend a relaxed morning or afternoon floating gentle Class II rapids, with time to swim and spot wildlife along the canyon walls. Ages 6 and up.

Equipment provided: raft, paddles, life jacket, helmet, dry bag.

Full Day Adventure

Our most popular trip. Paddle a full day of Class III rapids, then stop on a sandy beach for a riverside lunch prepared by your guides. Great for groups who want real whitewater without an overnight. Ages 12 and up.

Equipment provided: all river gear, wetsuit, splash jacket, lunch.

Big Mallard Challenge

Take on one of the river's most famous rapids. This adrenaline-packed day runs Class IV whitewater, including the giant waves of Big Mallard. Previous rafting experience is recommended. Ages 16 and up.

Equipment provided: all river gear, wetsuit, splash jacket, lunch.

Los Dientes del Diablo

Named for the jagged boulders known as "the devil's teeth," this technical rapid rewards teamwork and quick paddling. A two-day trip with a night of camping under the stars. Ages 14 and up.

Equipment provided: all river gear, tents, sleeping pads, all meals.

Grand Canyon Expedition

Our signature five-day journey through remote, roadless canyon country. Mix big rapids with calm stretches, hikes to hidden waterfalls, and camps on wide beaches. Ages 14 and up.

Equipment provided: all river and camping gear, all meals.
