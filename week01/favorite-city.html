Quebec City!

Quebec City is my favourite city because walking through it feels like stepping back in time. Old Quebec, with its narrow cobblestone streets and stone buildings, is a UNESCO World Heritage Site and one of the only fortified cities left in North America. The Château Frontenac towers over everything from its spot on the cliff, and from the Dufferin Terrace beside it you can look out over the St. Lawrence River for miles. My favourite part is the Petit-Champlain district, where little shops and cafés line the streets and the whole neighbourhood glows with lights in the evening. Everywhere you go, you can feel the city’s long history and its strong French culture.

LWhat makes Quebec City even more special is that it’s beautiful in every season. In the summer, people relax on the Plains of Abraham, watch street performers, and enjoy patios all over the old town. In the winter, the city turns into something out of a postcard, with snow on the rooftops and the famous Winter Carnival bringing ice sculptures, outdoor activities, and Bonhomme, the carnival’s snowman mascot. Just outside the city, Montmorency Falls is a great place to visit, since it’s even taller than Niagara Falls. Between the history, the food, the friendly people, and the views, Quebec City is a place I could visit again and again without ever getting tired of it.
