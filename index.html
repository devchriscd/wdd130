Canada

Canada is a country in northern North America, stretching from the Atlantic to the Pacific and north into the Arctic. It's the second-largest country in the world by area, made up of 10 provinces and 3 territories, with Ottawa as its capital. Canada is officially bilingual in English and French, and is known for its vast natural landscapes, including forests, mountains, lakes and tundra. Most of its population of about 41 million lives near the southern border with the United States, in cities such as Toronto, Montréal and Vancouver.
