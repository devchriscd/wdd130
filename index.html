Canada

Canada is the world's second-largest country, spanning North America from the Atlantic to the Pacific and Arctic oceans. It is famous for its vast natural beauty, including the Rocky Mountains, immense forests, and thousands of lakes. Its major multicultural cities include Toronto, Vancouver, and Montreal. As a bilingual democracy with English and French as official languages, Canada is globally recognized for its high quality of life and welcoming culture.
